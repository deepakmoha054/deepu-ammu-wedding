✦ Our Story ✦

Seven Years of Love

August 2016

A Chance Meeting in Pala 🌸

Fate brought two strangers together at a coaching class in Pala, Kottayam. What began as a simple hello between classmates slowly blossomed into a deep, cherished friendship — the kind that changes you forever.

🌸
🏍️

2016 – 2019

Best Friends & Open Roads 🏍️

Good friends became best friends. Through countless study sessions, shared dreams, and long rides on their beloved Royal Enfield Classic 350, their bond grew into something far deeper — a quiet, certain kind of love.

June 2019

Two Hearts, One Proposal 💌

They proposed to each other — almost simultaneously — as if their hearts had decided together without telling them. A moment neither had planned, yet both had always hoped for.

💌
📚

2019 – 2020

Together in Thiruvananthapuram 📚

Newly in love and still chasing their dreams, they continued their studies together in Thiruvananthapuram — sharing the city, the journey, and every ordinary moment that felt extraordinary.

2021

Ammu's Wings Take Flight 📮

Athira joined the Indian Postal Service and was posted in Kottayam, beginning her career with dedication and grace. Distance began to creep in — but love held fast.

📮

April 18, 2024

Deepu Takes Flight to Germany ✈️

On April 18th, 2024, Deepak moved to Aachen, Germany — thousands of miles apart, yet never truly separated. Late-night calls bridged continents, and love found a way across every time zone.

✈️
🏡

2024 & Beyond

The Hardest Conversations 🏡

Like many love stories rooted in Kerala, theirs too had its share of quiet anxieties and family hesitations. Two different families, two different hometowns — it took time, patience, and a whole lot of love to build bridges. But slowly, steadily, families that were once unsure became the warmest supporters, opening their hearts completely. Today, what was once a tender hope is celebrated by both families with joy, grace, and love.

🌍

2024 – 2026

Across Every Ocean 🌍

For two long years, they held on — through midnight messages, the ache of distances, and the warmth of knowing every sacrifice was for a future together. Ammu at Thiruvananthapuram. Deepu in Aachen. And they waited.

April 2026

Coming Home 🏠

After two years across the world, Deepak finally came home — back to Kerala, back to family, and back to the one who had waited with unwavering love. After almost seven years, the moment had finally arrived.

🏠
💍

June 27, 2026

The Wedding Morning 💍

On a golden morning, under the blessings of family and the fragrance of flowers, Deepu and Ammu tie the knot — a love story seven years in the making, finding its most beautiful chapter yet.
